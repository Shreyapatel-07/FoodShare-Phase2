ABOUT US

Making Every Meal Matter

FoodShare is a platform designed to connect food donors with people and organizations that can distribute surplus edible food to those who need it.

Restaurants, hotels, event organizers and households can share details about available food. NGOs and volunteers can use the information to coordinate food collection and distribution.

Our goal is to reduce food waste, encourage responsible consumption and support communities.

Our Mission

Reduce food waste and help ensure that surplus edible food reaches people who need it.
